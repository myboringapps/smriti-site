# smriti-site

Public pages for Smriti on iPhone, iPad and Android, served by GitHub Pages (live) at https://myboringapps.github.io/smriti-site/

- `index.html`: Home
- `free-and-plus.html`: what Free and Smriti Plus include
- `privacy.html`: Privacy Policy (App Store and Google Play "Privacy Policy URL")
- `terms.html`: Terms of Use (linked from the Plus screen and Settings)
- `support.html`: Support and FAQ (App Store and Google Play "Support URL")

Plain HTML and CSS, no build step. Edit a page, commit, push; GitHub Pages updates within a minute once it is switched on (Settings → Pages → main, root).

The pages follow `clocked-ios-site`. The Free and Plus table and the privacy and terms wording must match the app: if the app changes (what Plus includes, what is stored, any network use), change these pages in the same step. The privacy policy and terms are a developer's draft, not legal advice: have them reviewed before launch.
