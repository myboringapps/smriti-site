Free and Plus

Smriti is free for good: no time limit, no account and no ads. The daily verse, your streak and the whole Karma Yoga part of the Gita are yours to keep. Smriti Plus is how you support Smriti, and it opens the whole library, every widget, more themes and sync.

₹50 a month

or ₹350 a year, or ₹1,000 once to keep it for good. Cancel any time. About five cups of chai a month.

Prices in India, rounded to the nearest price the store offers. The App Store and Google Play show your exact price, in your currency, before you buy. Plus works on all your devices on the same store account; Family Sharing is on for iPhone.

Smriti is built for iPhone and iPad and for Android phones and tablets, with the same Free and Plus on each. Some things differ by phone: Lock Screen widgets are iPhone only, and iCloud sync is iPhone and iPad only. The apps are coming soon.
