Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-31 · Effective 31 October 2026. This policy covers Smriti and Smriti Plus on iPhone and iPad, and on Android phones and tablets.

The short version. Today Smriti has no account, no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and no server of ours. What you read, your streak, your favourites and your notes stay on your device. We do not collect, receive or share your personal information. The one exception is email: if you write to us, we get what you send.

1. Who we are

Smriti is made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). You can reach me at hdmyappsupport+smriti@gmail.com.

2. What Smriti keeps on your device

To work, Smriti saves on your device: which verses you have read and when, your streak and calendar, your favourites and learning list, the devata, theme, icon and reminder time you chose, and the stories you have marked as read. This stays in the app's private storage on your phone or tablet. It is not sent to us. To use it, Smriti reads it itself; there is no account to connect it to.

Smriti's widgets show a small summary (your streak, your vahana, and today's verse) which the app keeps in storage shared between the app and its widgets, on your device only.

3. What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, address or any identifier
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software today, ours or anyone else's
  • No record of what you read, on any server

Smriti does not connect to our servers, because there are none. We have no way to look at your reading, and none to give to anyone else.

4. iCloud, backups and your other devices

  • iPhone and iPad, Smriti Plus. If you turn on sync, Smriti stores your streak, history and saved items in your own private iCloud (Apple's CloudKit private database), so they follow you across your devices. Only you can read them with your Apple Account. We cannot see them. Apple's own terms and privacy policy apply to iCloud.
  • Device backups. Your phone or tablet may back up its apps and their data (for example to iCloud, to your computer, or on Android to your Google Account) if you have that turned on in your device settings. If so, Smriti's data can be part of that backup, handled by Apple or Google under their policies. Smriti does not control it.
  • Android sync. If Smriti later adds backup or sync on Android, this policy will be updated first.

5. Notifications

Reminders are made by your device at the time you choose, with no server involved. They do not contain a verse or anything personal. You can turn them off in your device settings.

6. Purchases

Smriti Plus is bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles your payment, your account and your receipts under their own policies. We never see your card or payment details. Smriti checks on your device that your purchase is valid; it does not send it to us, and we do not know who you are.

7. Email you send us

If you email us for support, or tap "Suggest a better translation" in the app (which opens your mail app with a message ready), your email goes through your own mail account to us. We receive your email address and whatever you write, and for a translation suggestion the verse reference and the translation shown. We use it only to reply and to improve Smriti, we do not sell or share it, and we keep it only as long as we need it. To have it deleted, write to us.

8. Sharing a verse

When you share a verse as a card or text, Smriti makes it on your device and hands it to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp or Instagram. What you share, and to whom, is up to you, and that app's own policy applies after you share.

9. Children

Smriti does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no accounts, no ads today and no links that take a child out of the app other than the share and mail options above, which a parent or guardian can switch off in the device settings.

10. Your choices

  • Delete everything on the device: use Reset progress in Settings, or delete the app.
  • Delete what is in iCloud: on iPhone or iPad, Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Smriti.
  • Delete an email we hold: write to us at the address above.

11. Store privacy labels

Today, in the App Store, Smriti's privacy label is "Data Not Collected". On Google Play, its data safety section says that Smriti collects no data and shares no data. If that changes, the labels change.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above, and if the change matters to how your information is used, the app will tell you. Smriti has no ads and no tracking today. We may add ads in the future and cannot promise they will stay out; if we do, we will update this policy first, and the store privacy labels with it.
