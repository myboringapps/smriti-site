Frequently asked questions

Getting started

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login.

How does the daily verse work?

Today's verse walks the Gita in order, one verse for each day you read, starting at 1.1. It stays the same all day. Open Pravah to read on as much as you like; your streak counts a day when you read a verse.

How do I add a widget?

iPhone and iPad: touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap + at the top, search for Smriti, choose a size and tap Add Widget. To change what it shows, touch and hold it and tap Edit Widget.

Android: touch and hold an empty spot on the home screen, tap Widgets, find Smriti and drag one onto the screen.

The widgets are in the app too: Me → Home Screen widgets shows every one at its real size.

What is a vahana?

A vahana is the animal a devata rides: Nandi the bull for Shiva, Mushika the mouse for Ganesha, Garuda the eagle for Vishnu. Smriti has 19. They join you as your streak grows, and collecting them is free.

Free and Plus

What is free, and what is Smriti Plus?

Today's verse, Pravah, your streak, the Karma Yoga chapters of the Gita, a taste of every other work, all 19 vahanas, your Ishta Devata, the small widgets and one daily reminder are free. Smriti Plus opens the whole library, every widget, more themes, the evening reminder, unlimited favourites and sync.

How do I restore my purchase?

On iPhone, open Smriti → Me → Settings → Smriti Plus → Restore Purchases. On Android, open Smriti Plus and tap Restore, or reopen the app while signed in to the Google account you bought with. If it does not come back, email us with your order number.

How do I cancel Smriti Plus?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Smriti. On Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → Smriti. Cancel at least a day before the renewal.

What happens if my subscription ends?

Nothing is deleted. Your streak, history and everything you saved stay. Plus content becomes a preview until you subscribe again.

The texts

Who wrote the translations?

They are written by AI for Smriti, in our own words, and marked as AI-generated. They have not yet been checked by a Sanskrit scholar, so please treat them as an aid. The Sanskrit comes from public-domain editions on Wikisource.

I found a mistake. What do I do?

Open the verse, tap Open, then Suggest a better translation, and your mail app will open with the verse already filled in. Or email us. We rewrite any accepted change in our own words.

Is the Ramayana or the Mahabharata complete?

No. Smriti holds the complete Bhagavad Gita and selected parts of the Ramayana, the Mahabharata and the other works, and adds more over time.

Your data

Where is my data?

On your device. On iPhone and iPad, Smriti Plus can keep it in your own private iCloud. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I start again from the beginning?

Me → Settings → Reset progress. This clears your streak, readings and saved items on this device.
