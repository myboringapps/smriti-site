3. The texts, the translations and the stories

  • The Sanskrit comes from public-domain editions on Sanskrit Wikisource. Volunteers type those pages, so they can contain typing slips, gaps or variant readings. Smriti shows them as found and marks the ones we know about. Numbering follows those pages and may differ from other editions.
  • The transliteration is made by the app from the Sanskrit by standard rules.
  • The translations, meanings, stories and scene cards are written by AI for Smriti, in our own words, and are labelled as AI-generated. They have not yet been checked by a Sanskrit scholar, and they can be wrong, incomplete or out of context. They are an aid to reading, not an authority. Where it matters to you, check a trusted scholar or edition.
  • Selected passages. Smriti holds the whole Bhagavad Gita but only selected parts of the Ramayana, the Mahabharata and the other works. We do not call any of them complete unless they are.
  • Illustrations. The vahanas, devatas and other characters are cute, friendly illustrations made for Smriti. They are not meant to mock, and not meant as objects of worship. If one troubles you, tell us.
